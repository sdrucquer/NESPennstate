Penn State’s oldest & largest entrepreneurship club

You don’t need
a startup to start.

NES is Penn State’s oldest and largest entrepreneurship club. There’s something every week: general body meetings at Happy Valley LaunchBox every other week, and THON meetings in between. We bring in founders and investors, take trips to see companies up close, and back members who are building their own thing. Some people show up with a business. Most show up curious. Both are welcome, from any major.

  • 30+people at a typical general body meeting
  • 200+members in the community
  • 2NYC trips, plus trips across the country
  • $27K+won by NES teams at Penn State’s Inc.U in 2026

Have an idea?

Where to take it next.

Penn State has real programs and real money for student founders. Not sure which one fits, or whether it’s open right now? Start with a conversation.

Always open

Talk to Happy Valley LaunchBox.

Fill out their contact form and they’ll set up a meeting and point you to the right mentor or program, whatever stage you’re at. Programs have deadlines; this doesn’t.

Contact LaunchBox

Or go straight to a program (check each one for current deadlines):

  • Test
    Idea TestLabFour weeks to talk to customers and test your idea, with coaching and up to $500.
  • Build
    MVP DevLab90 days to build and test a prototype, with up to $1,000 in support.
  • Launch
    FastTrack AcceleratorTen weeks of business-building help and up to $1,000 in milestone funding.
  • Pitch
    Inc.U CompetitionPitch for prize money. In 2026 the top three won $15,000, $10,000 and $5,000.
  • Go all in
    Summer FoundersA $15,000 equity-free grant to work on your startup full-time over the summer.
